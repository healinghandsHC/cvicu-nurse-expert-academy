CVICU Nurse Expert Academy

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 17, 2026

Summary: The app does not require an account, display advertising, use analytics or crash-reporting services, collect location or device identifiers, or transmit your learning activity to the developer. Learning progress is stored locally on your device.

Information stored by the app

CVICU Nurse Expert Academy stores learning progress and practice-answer results in local application storage on your device. This includes completed modules, whether a daily activity was marked complete, and whether locally answered practice questions were correct. This information is used only to display your progress and adapt practice inside the app.

Information we do not collect

The app does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, login credentials, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier, payment information, health records, patient information, or other personally identifying information. Do not enter protected health information or patient-identifying information into the app.

Transmission and sharing

The app does not transmit locally stored learning progress to the developer or share it with third parties. It contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, social tracking SDK, or third-party crash-reporting SDK.

Device permissions

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage-library, Bluetooth, health, or advertising-tracking permissions. Android may provide standard operating-system network capability to the embedded web runtime, but the packaged educational content does not require a network connection.

Data retention and deletion

Learning progress remains in local application storage until it is cleared by the app, the Android application data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Because this data is not sent to the developer, the developer cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

Educational and health-information notice

The app contains clinical education about cardiovascular critical care. Viewing educational health content does not mean the app collects personal health information. The app is an educational supplement and does not replace institutional policies, current clinical guidelines, credentialing, or professional supervision.

Children’s privacy

The app is intended for adult nursing learners and clinical professionals. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If future versions add accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, external services, or other data practices, this policy and the applicable app-store disclosures should be updated before release.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information listed on the app’s Google Play store listing or the repository that hosts this policy.
